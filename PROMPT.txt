Build a tiny browser game called USDC STACK: a cute white ghost (the make.fun mascot) slides left and right along the bottom of the screen catching gold USDC coins that fall from the sky, stacking each caught coin into a wobbling tower on its head. Arrow keys or mouse to move. A missed coin shakes the tower and costs a life — 3 misses and the tower topples. 60 seconds, score = coins stacked, combo bonus for streaks, bright playful colors, big readable score, start screen and game-over screen. Single self-contained HTML file.
